A letter, just for you

As you grow older, I hope life becomes kinder to you. Aku doa semoga segala urusan kau dipermudahkan, segala impian kau tercapai, and every little prayer you make in silence will find its way to you. I hope you’ll always find reasons to smile, even on the days when life feels a little too heavy.

Sometimes, I may not say it enough, but please know that your existence means so much to me. I’m proud of the person you’re becoming, and I’ll always be rooting for you, even when life takes us in different directions.

Aku tak tahu apa yang akan jadi pada kita in the future, how busy we’ll get, or how much our lives will change. But one thing I hope for is that no matter how far life takes us, we’ll never become strangers. I hope we’ll still have a little space in each other’s lives, to share our stories, celebrate our happiness, and be there for each other when things get hard.

Thank you for being more than just a friend. Thank you for being a part of my life that I’ll always be grateful for. If there’s one thing I wish for you, it’s that you’ll always be loved the way you deserve to be loved.

Happy birthday, bestie. Semoga tahun ini membawa lebih banyak kebahagiaan, ketenangan, rezeki and beautiful memories into your life. No matter what happens, I’ll always be thankful that out of all the people in this world, I got to know you and call you my bestfriend.

Here’s to more years of friendship, more memories, and a bond that I hope will last a lifetime.

I love you always. 🫶🏻🤍
